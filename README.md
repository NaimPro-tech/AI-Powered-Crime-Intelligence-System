# Description

Type of data:
Crime records in CSV format with numerical and textual values.

Data format:
CSV.

Number of samples:
6,574 instances.

Crimes considered:
Murder, Rape, Assault, Robbery, Kidnap, Body Found.

Feature Column:

incident_month: Month of commission of crime
incident_week: Week of commission of crime
incident_weekday: Day of a week of commision of crime
weekend: Holiday or Not
part_of_the_day: Specefic portion of a day
latitude & longitude: Latitude and longitude are imaginary lines drawn on maps and globes to help us find any exact spot on Earth using numbers
incident_place: Place where the incident occured
incident_district:  District of the crime incident
max_temp: Maximum temperature of a day
avg_temp: Average temperature of a day
min_temp: Minimum temperature of a day
weather_code: A set of numbers that represent specific atomospheric conditions
precip: Precipitation is any liquid or solid water that falls from the sky to the earth.
humidity: water vapor present in the air.
visibility: how clearly things can be seen or how far you can see
cloudcover: the portion/percentage of sky that covers with cloud
heatindex:  what the temperature feels like to the human body when relative humidity is combined with the air temperature
season: periods of the year
household: In this dataset it represent the totat number of homes in a specific area
male_population: Population of male in a area
female_population: Population of female in a area
total_population: Overall population in a area
gender_ration: statistical measure the proportion of males of females in a given population(this array is in Standard International Format) which represeants the number of males for every 100 females in each specific region
density_per_kmsq: Population density per square kilometers.
literacy_rate: Rate of education among population
religious_institution: Amount of Religious Institution
playground: Field or a place to play
park: A designed area of land kept in its natural or semi natural state for human enjoyment, recreation.
police_station: Total number of Police station
cyber_cafe: Total number of cyber cafe
school: The total number of schools
college: The total number of colleges
cinema: The total number of cinema hall
crime:  Types of crime.The target variable

Number of classes:
Six (corresponding to the crime categories).

Distribution of instances:
Varies across crime types based on real-world occurrences.

How data are acquired:
• Crime data collected from newspapers.We manually gather crime news articles over a seven-year period from a daily newspaper archive
• Socioeconomic data sourced from the National Census.
• Weather data retrieved from a Weather API.

Data source locations:
Bangladesh.

Where applicable:
Suitable for crime classification, forecasting, and crime analysis.
